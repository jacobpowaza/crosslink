## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-28

### Added

- Universal encrypted app-to-app connections over LAN, relay, and WebRTC.
- Device pairing, capability-based authorization, RPC, reconnect, and offline support.
- Node.js, browser, React, service, adapter, and conformance packages.
